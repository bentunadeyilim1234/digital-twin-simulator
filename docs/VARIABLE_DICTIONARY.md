## Definitions, measurement sources and notes

### ENV-01 · `E` · Environmental comfort (Çevresel konfor)

- **Definition (EN):** Aggregate share of occupied classroom/common-area hours in which temperature, humidity, CO2 and noise indicators fall within reference comfort ranges.
- **Tanım (TR):** Kullanılan sınıf ve ortak alan saatlerinde sıcaklık, nem, CO2 ve gürültü göstergelerinin referans konfor aralıklarında kaldığı sürenin toplulaştırılmış oranı.
- **Key in code:** `environmental_comfort`
- **Measurement:** % of occupied hours, reference bounds [0.0, 100.0], positive direction
- **Example aggregate sources:** Environmental sensor aggregates (temperature, humidity, CO2, noise); Facility comfort audits
  - component: CO2 concentration (mean, occupied hours) (ppm, [600.0, 1800.0], negative)
  - component: Noise level (mean, occupied hours) (dB(A), [35.0, 70.0], negative)
  - component: Thermal comfort compliance (%, [0.0, 100.0], positive)
- **References:** en16798, haverinen2011, barrett2015
- **Notes:** Physical condition indicator only. Never used as an indicator of individual behaviour or dangerousness.
